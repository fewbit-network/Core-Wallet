
# FewBit Core
### Decentralized. Fair. Built for the Future.

**FewBit (FBIT) is a community-driven blockchain built around real SmartNodes, Proof-of-Work mining, decentralized governance, and long-term sustainability.**

FewBit combines the security of mining with the services and governance of SmartNodes. Its economic model prioritizes the people who operate and maintain the network, while the SharedNode initiative opens participation to a broader community.

No premine. No centralized control over the network. A blockchain built by its community, for its community.

---

## 🌐 What Is FewBit?

FewBit is a hybrid blockchain designed to bring together miners, SmartNode operators, SharedNode participants, developers, and holders in one decentralized ecosystem.

Its architecture combines:

- ⚒️ **Proof-of-Work Mining** — GhostRider mining, adapted from Raptoreum, provides block production and network security through CPU/GPU mining.
- 🖥️ **SmartNodes** — Dedicated network nodes that support advanced services, governance, and blockchain security.
- 🤝 **SharedNode Participation** — A way for multiple users to contribute FBIT collectively and participate in shared SmartNode rewards.
- 🗳️ **On-chain Governance** — SmartNode operators can participate in decisions about network proposals and development funding.
- 🔐 **Advanced Network Security** — Dash-derived technologies, including ChainLocks, InstantSend/InstantLocks mechanisms, and Long-Lived Masternode Quorums (LLMQs), support the network's security and service architecture.
- 💰 **Sustainable Development Funding** — A transparent portion of block rewards supports continued development and maintenance.

FewBit is designed for long-term participation, transparent incentives, and a network that can evolve with its community.

## ⚒️ Proof-of-Work Mining

FewBit uses the **GhostRider algorithm**, adapted from Raptoreum, for CPU/GPU Proof-of-Work mining.

Mining is an essential part of the FewBit ecosystem. Miners compete to produce blocks and help keep transactions flowing across the network.

But FewBit takes a different approach to the relationship between mining and long-term participation.

Mining is the entry point. SmartNodes provide an additional way to participate in the network and earn rewards over time.

The objective is not simply to produce coins, but to build a distributed infrastructure that continues operating as the network grows.

## 🖥️ SmartNodes: The Core of FewBit

SmartNodes are a fundamental component of the FewBit network.

They provide essential network services and participate in the mechanisms that help protect the blockchain and support its decentralized operation.

SmartNode operators contribute infrastructure, maintain their nodes, and participate in network governance.

### Why SmartNodes Matter

- **Network infrastructure:** Distributed operators help maintain the services required by the ecosystem.
- **Security:** SmartNode-based quorum mechanisms and ChainLocks help protect the blockchain against certain chain reorganizations.
- **Governance:** Eligible operators can participate in on-chain voting.
- **Long-term incentives:** SmartNodes receive the dominant share of block rewards under the current reward model.

FewBit encourages real, independently operated SmartNodes rather than treating node participation as merely a passive investment product.

## 🤝 SharedNode: Expanding Participation

Not everyone can afford or operate an individual SmartNode. FewBit's SharedNode initiative is intended to make participation more accessible.

A SharedNode allows multiple users to contribute FBIT collectively, with participation in the rewards distributed according to the shared arrangement.

This model creates another way for community members to take part in the FewBit ecosystem without each participant having to operate a separate SmartNode.

### The SharedNode Vision

- **Accessible participation:** Users can contribute according to the available participation model.
- **Shared rewards:** Participants share rewards according to their contributions and the platform's rules.
- **Community growth:** More holders can become involved in the network's infrastructure.
- **Long-term alignment:** The model connects community participation with the operation of real SmartNode infrastructure.

The goal is to expand participation while keeping SmartNodes at the center of the FewBit ecosystem.

*SharedNode participation terms, contribution requirements, and reward calculations depend on the implementation of the service.*

## ⚖️ A New Reward Model

### The Block 600000 Upgrade

At block **600000**, FewBit introduced a major change to its block reward distribution.

The previous allocation of 65% to SmartNodes and 30% to miners was replaced with a model that places greater emphasis on the infrastructure securing and supporting the network.

### Current Block Reward Distribution

| Recipient | Allocation |
|---|---:|
| SmartNodes | 90% |
| Miners | 5% |
| Development Fund | 5% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

### Why This Matters

**90% SmartNode rewards**

SmartNodes receive the majority of block rewards, strengthening the economic incentives for operators who maintain the network's infrastructure.

**5% Mining rewards**

Mining remains an essential part of the protocol, providing Proof-of-Work block production and supporting the chain's operation.

**5% Development Fund**

A dedicated allocation provides resources for ongoing development, maintenance, and future improvements.

This model reflects FewBit's holder-first and infrastructure-focused economic direction: mining produces the blocks, SmartNodes support the network, and the Development Fund helps the project continue evolving.

## 🔥 Development Fund and Reward History

FewBit's funding model has evolved alongside the network.

- **Before the earlier reward transition:** A burn-based founder-reward mechanism was used.
- **From block 300300:** The network transitioned to a Development Fund allocation, replacing the previous burn-based approach.
- **From block 600000:** The reward distribution changed to 90% SmartNodes, 5% miners, and 5% Development Fund.

## 🔐 Security and Decentralized Infrastructure

FewBit combines Proof-of-Work with SmartNode-based network services to create a hybrid architecture.

Its Dash-derived technology stack includes mechanisms designed to support:

- **ChainLocks:** Quorum-based protection against certain blockchain reorganizations.
- **InstantSend/InstantLocks:** Transaction-locking mechanisms, where supported and active.
- **LLMQs and DKG:** Long-Lived Masternode Quorums and Distributed Key Generation for quorum-based services.
- **CoinJoin:** Privacy-oriented transaction mixing functionality inherited from the underlying technology stack.

These mechanisms serve different purposes and depend on the relevant protocol components being correctly configured and operational.

The combination of mining and distributed SmartNode infrastructure is a core part of FewBit's technical design.
